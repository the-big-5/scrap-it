# Product
## Concept [Locked]
ScrapIt is an iOS app that helps young adults (18–25, friend groups / university students) nurture friendships through shared photo experiences, collaborative digital scrapbooks, and social discovery, powered by CloudKit and personalized profile shelves.

## Goals
- Real-time shared capture sessions between friends, disposable-camera style: photos stay hidden until the session ends [Locked]
- Profile "shelves": a row of scrapbook covers on a virtual shelf [Locked]
- Collaborative digital scrapbooks [Draft]
- Social discovery [Draft]

## Non-goals
- Public feed
- Direct messaging
- (Not yet decided; do not build: Android/web, ads, in-app purchases, comments/reactions, push notifications)
